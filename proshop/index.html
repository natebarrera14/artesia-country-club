Artesia’s Local Golf Pro


The Jimenez ProShop at Artesia Country Club, led by Golf Pro Juan Jimenez, offers a wide variety of top-quality golf apparel, accessories, and equipment, including shirts, shorts, socks, shoes, and more. The ProShop also provides various services such as organizing tournaments, scheduling tee times, and offering golf lessons, making it a one-stop-shop for all your golfing needs.

Tee Times

Ready to hit the greens at Artesia Country Club?

Book your tee time today and experience the beauty of our 18-hole course designed by Ralph M. Plummer and George Williams. Whether you're a seasoned pro or just starting out, our course is perfect for golfers of all skill levels. Don't wait - reserve your tee time now and get ready for an unforgettable round of golf!

Golf Lessons

Improve your golf game with expert lessons from Juan Jimenez, the Golf Pro at Artesia Country Club! Whether you're a beginner looking to learn the basics or an experienced golfer seeking to fine-tune your skills, Juan's personalized instruction can help you reach your full potential. With years of experience and a passion for the sport, Juan will guide you every step of the way to improve your swing, accuracy, and overall technique. Book your lessons today and take your golf game to the next level!
