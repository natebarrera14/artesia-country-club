About Artesia CC

Artesia Country Club is a private golf course and social club established in 1946, located in Artesia, New Mexico. The course was designed by Ralph Plummer, a renowned golf course architect, and spans across 18 challenging holes. Plummer loved to plan and strategize, and during his career, he designed and redesigned 86 golf courses.

The Artesia Country Club has since become a local attraction for golfers of all skill levels and offers dining, casual and formal events, and business meetings at the Clubhouse. View our Board of Directors.
