# Memberships

## Looking for a place to relax, unwind, and enjoy quality time?

Bring your family and friends to Artesia Country Club. We have it all! With an 18-hole golf course, a luxurious clubhouse, and top-notch dining, our club is the perfect place to call your second home.

### Apply for Membership

Looking to join ACC? It’s an easy process. Just download our Membership Application, fill it out, and bring it in to the country club so we can start the process to get you added to the club roster.

[Download Membership Application](https://www.artesiacc.com/s/Artesia-Country-Club-New-Member-Application.pdf)

[Download Junior Membership Application](https://www.artesiacc.com/s/2025-JUNIOR-MEMBERSHIPS.doc)

### Join the Cart Club

Golfing is always more fun when you don’t have to walk a marathon in the process. Download our Cart Club Application and bring it in to ACC so we can get you set up with our Cart Club Membership.

[Download Cart Club Application](https://www.artesiacc.com/s/Artesia-Country-Club-Cart-Club-Contract.pdf)

### Have Your Own Cart?

No problem! Just fill out our Private Cart Agreement which outlines rules and regulations for the use of the cart, including safety guidelines, insurance requirements, and specific areas of the course where private carts are allowed to operate.

[New Private Cart License Agreement Coming Soon](https://www.artesiacc.com/memberships)

## Clubhouse Rules

The clubhouse rules provide guidelines for club members and their guests to use the club's facilities.

Members can invite non-resident guests, entertain resident guests for meals, and use the club for private parties.

## Bylaws

The Artesia Country Club Bylaws provide a framework of rules and procedures that establish the club's structure, purpose, and membership requirements. They outline how the club operates, including how decisions are made, how membership is granted or revoked, how disputes are resolved, and how finances are managed.

[View Club Bylaws](https://www.artesiacc.com/bylaws)

## Dress Code

The dress code at Artesia Country Club requires sport casual attire, with presentable clean collared shirts, and pants, or shorts for both the clubhouse and golf course. Sleeveless shirts with collars are allowed for women golfers, while men must wear collared shirts with sleeves.

[View Dress Code](https://www.artesiacc.com/dress-code)

## Golf Course Rules

To play on the Artesia Country Club golf course, members and guests must register in the Pro Shop, adhere to the dress code, and follow rules regarding golf carts, tee times, course etiquette, and behavior towards staff

[View Course Rules](https://www.artesiacc.com/clubhouse-rules-1)
