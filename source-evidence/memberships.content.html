Bylaws

The Artesia Country Club Bylaws provide a framework of rules and procedures that establish the club's structure, purpose, and membership requirements. They outline how the club operates, including how decisions are made, how membership is granted or revoked, how disputes are resolved, and how finances are managed.

View Club Bylaws

Dress Code

The dress code at Artesia Country Club requires sport casual attire, with presentable clean collared shirts, and pants, or shorts for both the clubhouse and golf course. Sleeveless shirts with collars are allowed for women golfers, while men must wear collared shirts with sleeves.

View Dress Code

Golf Course Rules

To play on the Artesia Country Club golf course, members and guests must register in the Pro Shop, adhere to the dress code, and follow rules regarding golf carts, tee times, course etiquette, and behavior towards staff
