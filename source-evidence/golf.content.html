About Our Course

The Artesia Country Club's stunning 18-hole golf course, was designed by renowned architects Ralph M. Plummer and George Williams. It features a par of 72 and 6,124 yards of Bermuda grass fairways and greens, this course offers both a challenging and enjoyable golfing experience. Our slope rating of 118 and course rating of 68.9 promise an exciting game for golfers of all skill levels. Come and enjoy a round of golf on our picturesque course, opened in 1946 and maintained to the highest standards by our expert grounds crew.

Dress Code

The dress code at Artesia Country Club requires sport casual attire, with presentable clean collared shirts, and pants, or shorts for both the clubhouse and golf course. Sleeveless shirts with collars are allowed for women golfers, while men must wear collared shirts with sleeves.

Golf Course Rules

To play on the Artesia Country Club golf course, members and guests must register in the Pro Shop, adhere to the dress code, and follow rules regarding golf carts, tee times, course etiquette, and behavior towards staff
