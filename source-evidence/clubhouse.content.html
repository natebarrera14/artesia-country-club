Dress Code

The dress code at Artesia Country Club requires sport casual attire, with presentable clean collared shirts, and pants, or shorts for both the clubhouse and golf course. Sleeveless shirts with collars are allowed for women golfers, while men must wear collared shirts with sleeves.

Clubhouse Rules

The clubhouse rules provide guidelines for club members and their guests to use the club's facilities.

Members can invite non-resident guests, entertain resident guests for meals, and use the club for private parties.
